Remember Your Creator Before It Is Too Late

Joshua M. Haub · July 30, 2026

“Remember also your Creator in the days of your youth, before the evil days come and the years draw near when you will say, ‘I have no delight in them.’” (Ecclesiastes 12:1, NASB95)

Ecclesiastes 12:1 is one of my favorite passages in the Bible. If there was one passage I would want on my tombstone, it would be this one.

The author of Ecclesiastes, in 11:9–12:1, discusses (in the NASB) three “R”s: Rejoice (11:9), Remove (11:10), and Remember (12:1). The author’s plea in 12:1 is for his readers to remember God. He has spent the entirety of the book discussing the vanity of life without Him!

A dear friend of mine once used this analogy: “Life is like a refrigerator; it will NEVER cook your food!” If we think life (without God, “under the sun”) will satisfy us, we are in for a sad awakening. No matter how much we try to find meaning and fulfillment in the things of this life (money, status, etc.), it will never provide us with what we want! Life without God is meaningless.

The author argues that life is not about “stuff” but about fearing God and obeying Him (12:13–14). So, he argues at the end of the book that we ought to enjoy life, but remember God. Enjoy the things you have but do not forget God will one day judge us, so live righteously.

But “remember God” is not simply a “recall to your mind.” Instead, it is a plea to serve Him. He says, “Remember God while you’re young before you get old.” The author wants us to start remembering (and obeying) right now!

For many of you, you have been serving God for a long time. Maybe not all of us served Him “in the days of our youth,” but once we came to recognize Him, we started. We realized the same thing the author did: “Life without God is meaningless!”

A lot of our world is recognizing the hopeless vanity of this life. Society is constantly asking, “What is the purpose for my life?” We make movies about it, TV shows seeking to answer it, influencers and motivational speakers call people to take up their “life’s motto.” Not to mention those who are no longer with us because the depression and pain of this broken world has led them down the darkest of paths. Our society is desperately sick for meaning and purpose. And if you have never experienced that weight of the vanity of life, then just wait, it is coming.

What is the answer? Where does meaning and purpose come from? How do I overcome the horrors of Ecclesiastes 1: “The sun keeps rising and setting, the earth continues on, and one day I will die and be forgotten”? Social media, video games, entertainment, and distractions will only hold the recognition of this at bay. You can only delay it for so long.

The answer? Remember God in the days of your youth. Right now.

There is a disappointment you may feel reading the book of Ecclesiastes, but there is also a hope. Despite the vanity of life without God, with Him there is great meaning! When we are following Him, there is purpose for my life! The things I do actually matter because I am serving Him and looking to the future, to a time where I will live with Him forever.

You can try to find meaning without Him, but it won’t lead where you want to go. A refrigerator will never cook your food. Money, status, relationships, will never give you what you can only find in God.

Evil days are coming. One day we will all grow old and die. What then? What will you do when you stand before God? Life may be temporary, but eternity is not. What will you do when the sun keeps rising and setting and one day you die and are forgotten? Will you honestly be able to say your life was anything more than meaningless searching? Did you even find what you were looking for?

Only with the Lord is there true meaning and purpose. Remember your Creator before it is too late.

To Him be the Glory.
