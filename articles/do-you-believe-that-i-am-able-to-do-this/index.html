Reflection

"Do you believe that I am able to do this?"

Joshua M. Haub · July 22, 2024

Here’s a few thoughts as I’ve been studying through Matthew this morning, feel free to add more! The more connections the better.

As you read the miracle stories in Matthew 8-9, you will find there are almost always 2 response: Faith and/or Disbelief

Those of faith

  • A Leper who believed (8:2-3)
  • A centurion who had great faith (8:10)
  • A demon-possessed man (8:29)
  • A group of friends and their paralytic friend (9:2)
  • A synagogue official (9:18)
  • A woman with a hemorrhage (9:20-22)

Those who disbelieved

  • The disciples (8:26-27)
  • Those who lived in Gadarenes (8:34)
  • Some of the scribes (9:3)
  • The mourners (9:24)

The text is making a stark contrast between the believers and the disbelievers. Then, here comes some blind men (9:27-30). They recognize Jesus (ironic, blind men who see better than those who could actually see): “Son of David! Have mercy on us!” was their cry (9:27, paraphrased). Jesus asks them the question that the text has been begging us to consider throughout chapters 8-9: “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” (9:28).

What will our answer be? Will we believe or disbelieve? Can Jesus really heal us if we have faith?

The blind men’s response: “Yes, Lord” (9:28). Based on FAITH, Jesus touched them and healed them (9:29).

If we have faith, Jesus can heal us.

May we be like those of faith in chapters 8-9, who know who Jesus is: Son of God (8:29) and Son of David (9:27). May we be like those who proclaimed “if I just touch Him, I WILL be healed” (9:21, paraphrased). May we be people who see ourselves as unworthy of Jesus, yet recognize His amazing authority (8:8-9). May we be people who recognize Jesus’ healing us is not a matter of “if He can” (cf. Mark 9:22-24) but as a matter of “if He is willing” (Matt 8:2). May we be people of GREAT FAITH who cause Jesus to marvel (9:10). And may we never be people who are of little faith and wonder: “who is this man?” (8:26-27). May we be people who feel secure because Jesus is in our boat as we go through the storms of life. If He can sleep soundly amid terrifying storms, so can we (8:24).

The question does not lie within Jesus’ ability to heal us, the question lies in if we are willing to believe in Him. What will our answer be? Do we believe Jesus is able to heal us? If we disbelieve and reject Him (8:34), He will get into His boat and leave us lost in our sins (9:1).

“Do you believe I am able to do this?”
“Yes, Lord [I BELIEVE!]”

To Him be the Glory.
